## 🌟 Course Highlights (V2 Board-Mastery Architecture)

1. **📖 Full 61-Chapter Course Reader (`705,843` words across 10 Organ-System Sections):**
   - Complete chapter text, `637` reconstructed clinical & molecular tables, `591` captioned dysmorphology/imaging/pathway figures, and standalone high-yield clinical pearls.
2. **🧬 Master Rare Disease Gene & OMIM Atlas (`2,590` Records):**
   - Header-aware mapping of syndromes, causative genes, inheritance patterns (`AD`, `AR`, `XLD`, `XLR`, `mtDNA`, `Imprinting / UPD`), and OMIM numbers: [`wiki/omim_gene_atlas.md`](wiki/omim_gene_atlas.md).
3. **🩺 Bedside Phenotype-to-Syndrome Differential Matcher ("Phenomizer Lite"):**
   - Click 1 or more of **28 bedside phenotypic categories** or type clinical/imaging signs (`eye-of-the-tiger`, `molar tooth`, `cherry-red spot`, `macroglossia`) to rank matching rare syndromes across all 61 chapters.
4. **💊 Master Precision Therapies & Orphan Drug Matrix (`361` Entries):**
   - Enzyme replacement therapies (ERTs), gene therapies, antisense oligonucleotides, small-molecule targeted therapies, and surveillance protocols: [`wiki/precision_therapies_matrix.md`](wiki/precision_therapies_matrix.md).
5. **🃏 1,693 Active-Recall Flashcards + Anki Master Deck:**
   - Study in the 3D Web App with keyboard shortcuts (`Space`, `←/→`, `1` Needs Review, `2` Mastered) or import [`anki_vikkypaedia_rare_disease_deck.tsv`](anki_vikkypaedia_rare_disease_deck.tsv) directly into Anki.
6. **📝 850 Blind Board-Style MCQs (5 Archetypes):**
   - Strictly masks the target syndrome, acronym, and gene symbol from the question stem and provides option-by-option distractor rationales.
7. **🖼️ Dysmorphology, Neuroimaging & Pathway Visual Spotter (`591` Captioned Figures):**
   - Toggle **Spotter Self-Test Mode** to blur captions and test visual diagnosis.
