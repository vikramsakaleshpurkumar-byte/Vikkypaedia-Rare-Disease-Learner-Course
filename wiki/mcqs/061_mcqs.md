### Question 1 (Clinical Vignette & Syndrome Diagnosis)
A pediatric patient is evaluated in the medical genetics clinic with the following clinical and molecular profile: Inheritance: XL — Ig: All isotypes decreased in majority of patients; some patients have detectable immunoglobulins — Associated Features: Severe bacterial infections, normal numbers of pro-B cells. Which of the following rare disorders is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** BTK deficiency, X-linked agammaglobulinemia
- **B.** Hoffman syndrome/ TOP2B deficiency
- **C.** UNG deficiency
- **D.** INO80 deficiency

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: A** — BTK deficiency, X-linked agammaglobulinemia

**Rationale:** **BTK deficiency, X-linked agammaglobulinemia** caused by pathogenic variants in **`BTK`** presents with: Inheritance: XL — Ig: All isotypes decreased in majority of patients; some patients have detectable immunoglobulins — Associated Features: Severe bacterial infections, normal numbers of pro-B cells.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **Hoffman syndrome/ TOP2B deficiency** is incorrect (associated with TOP2B — Inheritance: AD — Associated Features: Recurrent infections, facial dysmorphism, limb anomalies). **UNG deficiency** is incorrect (associated with UNG — Inheritance: AR — Ig: IgG and IgA decreased, IgM increased — Associated Features: Enlarged lymph nodes and ger). **INO80 deficiency** is incorrect (associated with INO80 — Inheritance: AR — Ig: IgG and IgA decreased, IgM increased — Associated Features: Severe bacterial infections).

</details>
