### Question 1 (Clinical Vignette & Syndrome Diagnosis)
A pediatric patient is evaluated in the medical genetics clinic with the following clinical and molecular profile: the causative gene Chondrodysplasias. Which of the following rare disorders is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** NOS 04-0030
- **B.** NOS 09-0100
- **C.** NOS 13-0140
- **D.** Group 1

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: D** — Group 1

**Rationale:** **Group 1** caused by pathogenic variants in **`FGFR3`** presents with: FGFR3 Chondrodysplasias.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **NOS 04-0030** is incorrect (associated with SLC26A2 — Diastrophic dysplasia, SLC26A2-related — Inheritance: AR). **NOS 09-0100** is incorrect (associated with COL9A3 — Stickler syndrome, recessive type, COL9A3-related — Inheritance: AR). **NOS 13-0140** is incorrect (associated with DDR2 — SEMD, short limb–abnormal calcification type, DDR2-related — Inheritance: AR — Notes: See also other dysplasia).

</details>
