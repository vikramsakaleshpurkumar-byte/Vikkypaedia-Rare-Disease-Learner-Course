### Question 1 (Clinical Vignette & Syndrome Diagnosis)
A pediatric patient is evaluated in the medical genetics clinic with the following clinical and molecular profile: Primary Biomarker: the causative gene. Which of the following rare disorders is the most likely diagnosis?

- **A.** Mitochondrial disorder
- **B.** Propionic acidemia (PA)
- **C.** Glycogen storage disease type XIII
- **D.** X-linked adrenoleukodystrophy

<details><summary><b>Show Answer & Rationale</b></summary>

**Correct Answer: B** — Propionic acidemia (PA)

**Rationale:** **Propionic acidemia (PA)** caused by pathogenic variants in **`C3`** presents with: Primary Biomarker: C3.

**Why Other Options Are Incorrect:** **Mitochondrial disorder** is incorrect (associated with MTCO1 — COI (MTCO1) — Kinase Levels: Normal — Inheritance: Maternal inheritance — Trigger for Rhabdomyolysis: Prolonge). **Glycogen storage disease type XIII** is incorrect (associated with ENO3 — Normal High — Pattern of Inheritance: AR — Trigger for Rhabdomyolysis: Aerobic and anaerobic exercise, symptom). **X-linked adrenoleukodystrophy** is incorrect (associated with ABCD1 — Clinical Features: Behavioral abnormalities (e.g., ADHD), auditory processing abnormalities, spasticity,+/− ad).
