### [Chapter 32: Pediatric Intestinal Pseudo-Obstruction](../chapters/032_Pediatric_Intestinal_Pseudo_Obstruction.md)

### [Chapter 33: Rare Inborn Defects Causing Malabsorption](../chapters/033_Rare_Inborn_Defects_Causing_Malabsorption.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Deficiency of one or more of the brush border’s disaccharidases or oligosaccharidases may cause diarrhea, bloating, and flatulence after the ingestion of a sugar.
- **Key Genes:** `ABCC8`, `ADA`, `AICDA`, `ATP7A`, `BLNK`, `BTK`, `CD19`, `CD20`, `CD3`, `CD40`, `CD40L`, `CD45`
- **OMIM IDs:** `#126650`, `#601330`, `#613217`, `#616868`
