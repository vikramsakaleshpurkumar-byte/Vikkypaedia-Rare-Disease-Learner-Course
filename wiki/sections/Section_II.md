### [Chapter 7: Ciliopathies: Clinical Presentations and Syndromes](../chapters/007_Ciliopathies_Clinical_Presentations_and_Syndromes.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** The term ciliopathy describes a broad spectrum of mostly autosomal recessive genetic diseases, which are due to defects in primary cilium function or formation, an evolutionary conserved hair-like cell organelle.
- **Key Genes:** `ADAMTS9`, `AHI1`, `ALMS1`, `ANKS6`, `ARL13B`, `ARL3`, `ARL6`, `ARMC9`, `BBS1`, `BBS10`, `BBS12`, `BBS2`
### [Chapter 8: Mosaic Overgrowth Syndromes](../chapters/008_Mosaic_Overgrowth_Syndromes.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** PIK3CA -related overgrowth spectrum is the most common mosaic overgrowth disorder and is up to 1,000 times more common than the next most prevalent mosaic overgrowth disorder, Proteus syndrome.
- **Key Genes:** `AKT1`, `AKT2`, `AKT3`, `ATK1`, `KRAS`, `MPPH2`, `MTOR`, `PIK3CA`, `PIK3CD`, `PIK3CG`, `PIK3R1`, `PTEN`
