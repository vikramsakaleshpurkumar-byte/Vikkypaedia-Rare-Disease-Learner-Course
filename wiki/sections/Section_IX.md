## Chapter Synthesis & Genomic Highlights
### [Chapter 49: Nonimmune Hemolytic Anemias](../chapters/049_Nonimmune_Hemolytic_Anemias.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** The hallmark of NIHA is a negative antihuman immunoglobulin test (i.e., direct Coombs test).
- **Key Genes:** `ADA`, `ADAMTS13`, `ANK1`, `EPB42`, `G6PD`, `HBA1`, `HBA2`, `KCNN4`, `PIEZO1`, `PKLR`, `SLC2A1`, `SLC4A1`
### [Chapter 50: Lymphoproliferative Disorders](../chapters/050_Lymphoproliferative_Disorders.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Langerhans cell histiocytosis (LCH), while rare (incidence of approximately 5 per million children), is the most common of the histiocytic disorders in children (Table 50.1).
- **Key Genes:** `ALK`, `AP3B1`, `BRAF`, `BRAFV600E`, `CASP10`, `CASP8`, `CD163`, `CD27`, `CD68`, `CP`, `FAS`, `FASLG`
