## Chapter Synthesis & Genomic Highlights
### [Chapter 53: When to Consider a Primary Immune Deficiency Disorder: Pathogens Associated With Specific Immune Defects](../chapters/053_When_to_Consider_a_Primary_Immune_Deficiency_Disorder_Pathogens_Asso.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** The hallmark feature of IEIs is the immune system’s inability to mount an effective defense against infections, rendering affected individuals highly vulnerable to recurrent and severe infectious diseases.
- **Key Genes:** `ADA`, `AIRE`, `AP3B1`, `ATG4`, `ATG4A`, `ATM`, `BTK`, `C1QA`, `C1QB`, `C1QC`, `C2`, `C3`
### [Chapter 54: Primary T Cell Immunodeficiencies](../chapters/054_Primary_T_Cell_Immunodeficiencies.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** SCID is characterized by near complete absence of peripheral T cells and impaired B cell function with or without reduction in B cell numbers.
- **Key Genes:** `ACP5`, `ADA`, `AK2`, `ATM`, `BCL10`, `BCL11B`, `BIRC4`, `CARD11`, `CD25`, `CD3D`, `CD3E`, `CD3Z`
### [Chapter 55: Innate Defects in Host Defenses Against Infections](../chapters/055_Innate_Defects_in_Host_Defenses_Against_Infections.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** AD, Autosomal dominant; AR, autosomal recessive; PA, pseudoautosomal recessive; XL, X-linked.
- **Key Genes:** `ABCA3`, `ACT1`, `ADA`, `AIRE`, `APOL1`, `ATG4`, `ATG4A`, `BAPX1`, `CARD9`, `CCR2`, `CD16`, `CD28`
### [Chapter 56: Rare and Unusual Etiologies of Atopic Diseases (Eczema, Urticaria)](../chapters/056_Rare_and_Unusual_Etiologies_of_Atopic_Diseases_Eczema_Urticaria.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Eczematous eruptions are characterized by scaly pink papules and plaques, which are typically pruritic.
- **Key Genes:** `ADA`, `CD25`, `DOCK8`, `FOXP3`, `IL2RA`, `IL2RG`, `MVK`, `NLRP3`, `PGM3`, `PLCG2`, `RAG1`, `RAG2`
### [Chapter 57: Hereditary Autoinflammatory Disorders](../chapters/057_Hereditary_Autoinflammatory_Disorders.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Systemic autoinflammatory diseases are rare genetic disorders characterized by recurrent episodes or continuous inflammation without evidence of autoimmunity.
- **Key Genes:** `ACP5`, `ADA2`, `ADAR1`, `ARPC1B`, `CDC42`, `CECR1`, `CIAS1`, `DNASE1`, `FAM105B`, `HA20`, `HOIL1`, `IFIH1`
### [Chapter 58: Type I Interferonopathies](../chapters/058_Type_I_Interferonopathies.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** Autoimmune manifestations and autoantibodies may also be noted; some patients fulfil diagnostic criteria for specific autoimmune disorders such as SLE.
- **Key Genes:** `ACP5`, `ADAR1`, `AIRE`, `ATAD3A`, `C1QA`, `C1QB`, `C1QC`, `C2`, `C3`, `DDX58`, `DNASE1`, `DNASE1L3`
### [Chapter 59: Disorders of Immune Regulation](../chapters/059_Disorders_of_Immune_Regulation.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** One of the most common manifestations is the development of autoimmune cytopenias , which can result in anemia, thrombocytopenia, neutropenia, or lymphopenia.
- **Key Genes:** `AIRE`, `CD25`, `CTLA4`, `FOXP3`, `IKZF2`, `IKZF3`, `IKZF5`, `IL10`, `IL10RA`, `IL10RB`, `IL2RA`, `IL2RB`
### [Chapter 60: Complement Deficiencies](../chapters/060_Complement_Deficiencies.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** The inheritance of complement deficiencies is autosomal recessive in all conditions, except for properdin and C1 inhibitor deficiencies with X-linked recessive and autosomal dominant inheritance, respectively.
- **Key Genes:** `C2`, `C3`, `C4A`, `C4B`, `C5`, `C6`, `C7`, `C9`, `CD46`, `CD55`, `CD59`, `CFB`
### [Chapter 61: Antibody Deficiencies](../chapters/061_Antibody_Deficiencies.md)
- **Core Clinical Pearl:** PAD is the most common type of inborn error of immunity (IEI) diagnosed, representing about half of all IEI cases.
- **Key Genes:** `AICDA`, `ARHGEF1`, `ATP6AP1`, `BLNK`, `BTK`, `CD154`, `CD19`, `CD20`, `CD21`, `CD40`, `CD40LG`, `CD79A`
