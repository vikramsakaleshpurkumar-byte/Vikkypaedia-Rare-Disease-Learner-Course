Beighton score. The range of motion of several key small and large joints is measured to provide an overview of joint hypermobility. Instability is not assessed. Scoring: 2 points for each bilateral measure in Nos. 1 to 4 and 1 point for No. 5, equaling a total possible score of 9. Hypermobility is considered significant with a score of ≥6 between the ages of 6 and 35. (Modified from Smits-Engelsman B, Klerks M, Kirby A.
