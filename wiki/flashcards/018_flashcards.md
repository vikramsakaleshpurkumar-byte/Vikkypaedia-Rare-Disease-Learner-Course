### Card 18 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the clinical evaluation of **HSAN2D/CIP Caused by Variants in SCN9A**, how is **paroxysmal extreme pain disorder** defined and characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
paroxysmal extreme pain disorder , primary erythromelalgia , and small fiber neuropathy (SFN) , whereas loss-of-function variants , which produce non-sodium currents, in both copies of SCN9A have been linked to channelopathy associated with congenital insensitivity to pain (CIP) .

</details>

### Card 19 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the clinical evaluation of **HSAN2D/CIP Caused by Variants in SCN9A**, how is **familial erythromelalgia** defined and characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
familial erythromelalgia and paroxysmal extreme pain disorder are the opposite of the loss-of-function variants observed in CIP, and are characterized by localized extreme periodic pain and autonomic manifestations such as skin flushing and increased temperature because of vasodilation.

</details>

### Card 20 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the clinical evaluation of **HSAN3 Caused by Variants in IKBKAP**, how is **familial dysautonomia (FD)** defined and characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
familial dysautonomia (FD) or the Riley-Day syndrome , is an autosomal recessive disorder which presents in infancy and mainly in Ashkenazi Jewish populations.

</details>

### Card 21 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the clinical evaluation of **HSAN3 Caused by Variants in IKBKAP**, how is **dysautonomic crisis** defined and characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
dysautonomic crisis episodes, which are characterized by nausea and vomiting, agitation, tachycardia, and hypertension, that are often triggered by emotional or physical stress.

</details>

### Card 22 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)

</details>

### Card 23 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the clinical evaluation of **HSAN7 Caused by Variants in SCN11A**, how is **small fiber neuropathy** defined and characterized?</summary>

small fiber neuropathy , causing pain, tingling, and numbness in distal arms and legs, sometimes associated with gastroparesis.

</details>
