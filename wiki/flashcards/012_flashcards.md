### Card 12 (Molecular Etiology)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> Pathogenic variants in **`TSC1`** are associated with which clinical phenotype and molecular mechanism in **TSC1 and TSC2**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Disruption of TSC1 or TSC2 , known tumor suppressor genes, produces the tuberous sclerosis complex (TSC) , a genetic disorder characterized by excessive growth or malformation of tissue throughout the body.

</details>

### Card 13 (Molecular Etiology)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> Pathogenic variants in **`SETD2`** are associated with which clinical phenotype and molecular mechanism in **SETD2**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Disruption of SETD2 can produce a neurodevelopmental disorder that includes macrocephaly (with or without associated ventriculomegaly), obesity, overgrowth, ASD, intellectual disability, developmental delay, and recurrent ear infections.

</details>

### Card 14 (Molecular Etiology)

DYRK1A variants produce a syndrome characterized by microcephaly, intellectual impairment, and in ~85% of affected individuals, ASD.

</details>

### Card 26 (Differential Diagnosis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the differential diagnosis of **###### Severity Levels for Autism Spectrum Disorder (Examples of Level of Support Needs)—cont’d**, what are the distinguishing features of **Level 2 “Requiring substantial support”**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**p p**: Marked deficits in verbal and nonverbal social communication skills; social impairments apparent even with supports in place; limited initiation of social interactions; and reduced or abnormal responses to social overtures from others. For example, a person who speaks simple sentences, whose interaction is limited to narrow special interests, and who has markedly odd nonverbal communication.; **pp**: Inflexibility of behavior, difficulty coping with change, or other restricted/repetitive behaviors appear frequently enough to be obvious to the casual observer and interfere with functioning in a variety of contexts. Distress and/or difficulty changing focus or action.

</details>

### Card 27 (Differential Diagnosis)

**Answer:**
**p p**: Without supports in place, deficits in social communication cause noticeable impairments. Difficulty initiating social interactions, and clear examples of atypical or unsuccessful responses to social overtures of others. May appear to have decreased interest in social interactions. Difficulty switching between activities. Problems of organization and planning hamper independence.

</details>
