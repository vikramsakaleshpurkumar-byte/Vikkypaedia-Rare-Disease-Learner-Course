### Card 19 (Differential Diagnosis)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the differential diagnosis of **Clai ssification of Hearing Loss**, what are the distinguishing features of **Onset**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**• Conductive hearing loss, arising from abnormalities of the external ear and/or the ossicles of the middle ear (and rarely from structural abnormalities of the inner ear) • Sensorineural hearing loss, due to malfunction of the inner ear structures arising from abnormalities of the cochlea or auditory nerve • Mixed hearing loss, a combination of conductive and sensorineural hearing loss • Central auditory dysfunction or auditory neuropathy spectrum disorder, from damage or dysfunction at the level of the eighth cranial nerve (auditory nerve), auditory brain stem, or cerebral cortex.**: • Congenital • Prelingual • Postlingual • Adult onset and presbycusis (age-related hearing loss)

</details>
