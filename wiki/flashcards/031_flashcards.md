### Card 12 (Molecular Etiology)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> Pathogenic variants in **`IKBKG`** are associated with which clinical phenotype and molecular mechanism in **Epithelial Barrier Defects**?</summary>

### Card 13 (Molecular Etiology)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> Pathogenic variants in **`COL7A1`** are associated with which clinical phenotype and molecular mechanism in **Epithelial Barrier Defects**?</summary>

**Answer:**
COL7A1 encodes for collagen VII; pathogenic variants lead to an autoimmune response and deficiency in anchoring fibrils.

</details>

### Card 14 (Molecular Etiology)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> Pathogenic variants in **`FERMT1`** are associated with which clinical phenotype and molecular mechanism in **Epithelial Barrier Defects**?</summary>

**Answer:**
FERMT1 disruptions effect phosphoprotein and focal adhesion adaptor component kindling-1 leading to an inflammatory response in keratinocytes.

</details>

### Card 15 (Molecular Etiology)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> Pathogenic variants in **`GUCY2C`** are associated with which clinical phenotype and molecular mechanism in **Epithelial Barrier Defects**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Deficiencies in the two genes, guanylate cyclase 2C (GUCY2C) and solute carrier 9A3 (SLC9A3) , are linked to congenital sodium diarrhea (CSD) and also to the development of an IBD phenotype.

</details>

### Card 20 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the clinical evaluation of **Terminology, Definition, and Evaluation of VEO-IBD**, how is **Very early onset inflammatory bowel disease (VEO-IBD)** defined and characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Very early onset inflammatory bowel disease (VEO-IBD) is a descriptive term arbitrarily assigned to a subgroup of pediatric IBD with onset <6 years of age.

</details>

### Card 21 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the clinical evaluation of **Terminology, Definition, and Evaluation of VEO-IBD**, how is **Pediatric IBD** defined and characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Pediatric IBD patients age ≥6 years are believed to have the common form of IBD and share the same etiology and clinical presentation with adult onset IBD.

</details>

### Card 22 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the clinical evaluation of **Terminology, Definition, and Evaluation of VEO-IBD**, how is **infantile** defined and characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
infantile inflammatory bowel disease with onset <2 years of age and neonatal onset inflammatory bowel disease with onset <28 days of age.

</details>

### Card 23 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the clinical evaluation of **Imaging**, how is **Severe combined immunodeficiency (SCID)** defined and characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Severe combined immunodeficiency (SCID) presents with epithelial cell apoptosis, and eosinophilic or neutrophilic infiltrates with villous atrophy.

</details>

### Card 24 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the clinical evaluation of **Imaging**, how is **Chronic granulomatous disease (CGD)** defined and characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Chronic granulomatous disease (CGD) has a prevalence of noncaseating granulomas without significant surrounding inflammation, eosinophils, or pigmented macrophages.

</details>
