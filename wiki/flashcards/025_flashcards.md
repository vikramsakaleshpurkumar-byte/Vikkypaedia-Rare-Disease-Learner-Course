### Card 18 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the clinical evaluation of **Clinical Features**, how is **four hallmark clinical criteria** defined and characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
four hallmark clinical criteria for PCD are (1) unexplained neonatal respiratory distress in term infants, (2) early-onset moist cough which is present year-round, (3) early-onset chronic nasal congestion, and (4) laterality defects.

</details>

### Card 19 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)

**Answer:**
Unexplained neonatal respiratory distress , especially in term infants or lasting longer than 5 days, is a common manifestation of PCD, and is present in 80% of children with PCD.

</details>
