### Card 9 (Molecular Etiology)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> Pathogenic variants in **`CACNA1S`** are associated with which clinical phenotype and molecular mechanism in **Hypokalemic Periodic Paralysis (HypoPP)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
It is due primarily to pathogenic variants in the CACNA1S gene, which codes for a voltage-gated calcium channel that interacts with the ryanodine receptor to prompt calcium release from the sarcoplasmic reticulum.

</details>

### Card 10 (Molecular Etiology)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> Pathogenic variants in **`KCNJ2`** are associated with which clinical phenotype and molecular mechanism in **Andersen-Tawil Syndrome (ATS)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
It is due to pathogenic variants in KCNJ2 expressed in both skeletal and cardiac muscle as well as having a role in bony development.

</details>

### Card 11 (Molecular Etiology)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> Pathogenic variants in **`CLCN1`** are associated with which clinical phenotype and molecular mechanism in **Myotonia Congenita (MC)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
It is due to pathogenic variants in CLCN1 which codes for the voltage-gated chloride channel ClC-1, which has an essential role in resetting the sarcolemma after depolarization and muscle contraction.

</details>

### Card 18 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the clinical evaluation of **Andersen-Tawil Syndrome (ATS)**, how is **Dysmorphic features** defined and characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
