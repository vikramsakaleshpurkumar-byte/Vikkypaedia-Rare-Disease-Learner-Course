### Card 1 (Syndrome & OMIM Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the genetic etiology, inheritance pattern, and hallmark clinical features of **Duchenne muscular dystrophy (DMD)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Causative Gene(s):** `DMD` · **Inheritance:** Genetic

**Clinical & Molecular Profile:** Gene Product: Dystrophin — Cardiomyopathy: Dilated — ECG: Short PR interval, prolonged QT interval, increased QT:PT ratio, right ventricular hypertrophy, deep Q waves II, III, aVF, V5, V6 — Arrhythmia: Increased baseline HR, decreased rate variability, premature ventricular beats

</details>

**Answer:**
