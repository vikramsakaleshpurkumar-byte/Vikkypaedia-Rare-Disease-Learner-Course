### Card 1 (Syndrome & OMIM Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the genetic etiology, inheritance pattern, and hallmark clinical features of **Ataxia-telangiectasia**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Causative Gene(s):** `ATM` · **Inheritance:** AR

**Clinical & Molecular Profile:** Inheritance: AR — Leukemia, Hodgkin lymphoma, non-Hodgkin lymphoma — Other Clinical Features: Cerebellar ataxia, oculomotor apraxia, choreoathetosis, telangiectasia of conjunctivae, immune deficiency

</details>

### Card 2 (Syndrome & OMIM Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the genetic etiology, inheritance pattern, and hallmark clinical features of **Beckwith-Wiedemann syndrome**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Causative Gene(s):** `CDKN1C, LIT1` · **Inheritance:** AD

**Clinical & Molecular Profile:** Genetic Locus: 11p15: epigenetics and genomic alterations; CDKN1C, LIT1 — Inheritance: Imprinting, AD — Wilms tumor, hepatoblastoma, neuroblastoma, rhabdomyosarcoma — Other Clinical Features: Macrosomia, macroglossia, visceromegaly, omphalocele, renal abnormalities, neonatal hypoglycemia, hemihypertrophy

</details>

### Card 12 (Molecular Etiology)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> Pathogenic variants in **`SH2D1A`** are associated with which clinical phenotype and molecular mechanism in **Susceptibility Genes and Inheritance Patterns**?</summary>

</details>

### Card 13 (Molecular Etiology)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> Pathogenic variants in **`SDHAF2`** are associated with which clinical phenotype and molecular mechanism in **Hereditary Pheochromocytoma-Paraganglioma**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Hereditary pheochromocytoma-paraganglioma arises from pathogenic germline variants in several genes ( SDHA, SDHB, SDHC, SDHD, SDHAF2, TMEM127, MAX, and probably other rare genes like SLC25A11 , DNMT3A, and DLST ).

</details>

### Card 20 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the clinical evaluation of **MEN2A—Sipple Syndrome**, how is **Familiar medullary thyroid cancer (FMTC)** defined and characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Familiar medullary thyroid cancer (FMTC) has been classified as a variant of MEN2A, which requires at least two generations affected by RET germline variants or two members affected within the same generation.

</details>

### Card 21 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)

**Answer:**
ganglioneuromatosis or mucosal neuromas , one of the earlier and specific features; other unspecific features include marfanoid habitus, pectus excavatum, scoliosis, inability to produce tears (85% of patients), constipation, and diarrhea.

</details>

Hyperparathyroidism (PHPT) related to adenoma or hyperplasia of the parathyroid, is the most common endocrinopathy.

</details>
