### Card 14 (Molecular Etiology)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> Pathogenic variants in **`TP53RK`** are associated with which clinical phenotype and molecular mechanism in **Galloway-Mowat Syndrome**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Recessive variants in the genes encoding the four subunits of the KEOPS complex ( OSGEP , TP53RK , TPRKB , and LAGE3 ), which plays an important role in the brain and renal development, have also been identified in 37 affected individuals from 32 families.

</details>

### Card 16 (Molecular Etiology)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> Pathogenic variants in **`ITGB4`** are associated with which clinical phenotype and molecular mechanism in **Herlitz Junctional Epidermolysis Bullosa**?</summary>
