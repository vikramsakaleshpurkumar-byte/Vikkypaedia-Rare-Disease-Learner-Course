### Card 19 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the clinical evaluation of **Extraocular Movements**, how is **Horizontal saccade palsy** defined and characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Horizontal saccade palsy is a characteristic finding in the chronic form of neuronopathic Gaucher disease (Gaucher disease type III), whereas vertical saccade deficiency/vertical supranuclear gaze palsy is observed in patients with Niemann-Pick C (NPC) disease.

</details>
