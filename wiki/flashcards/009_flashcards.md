### Card 13 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the clinical evaluation of **Ectodermal Dysplasia**, how is **Ectodermal dysplasias (EDs)** defined and characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Ectodermal dysplasias (EDs) are genetically and phenotypically diverse disorders affecting two or more structures derived from the ectoderm, including teeth, skin, and ectodermal appendages such as hair, nails, eccrine sweat glands, and sebaceous glands.

</details>

**Answer:**
Hypohidrotic ectodermal dysplasias (HEDs) are phenotypically similar but genetically heterogeneous.
