### Card 1 (Syndrome & OMIM Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the genetic etiology, inheritance pattern, and hallmark clinical features of **Stargardt disease; 248200**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Causative Gene(s):** `ABCA4` · **Inheritance:** AR

**Clinical & Molecular Profile:** Mode of Inheritance: Autosomal recessive — Stargardt disease is a genetic retinal disorder caused by variants in ABCA4 which is crucial for the transport of vitamin A derivatives in photoreceptor cells of the retina.
