### Card 6 (Syndrome & OMIM Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the genetic etiology, inheritance pattern, and hallmark clinical features of **Friedreich ataxia**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Causative Gene(s):** `GAA` · **Inheritance:** AR

**Clinical & Molecular Profile:** Caudal to rostral ataxia progression, posterior column sensory loss, absent deep tendon reflexes, bilateral Babinski sign, ocular fixation instability; later stage vision and hearing loss. — Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy (75%); Scoliosis, pes cavus (60– 80%); Diabetes or glucose intolerance (20%) — Laboratory Features: ECG—non-specific ST-T

</details>
