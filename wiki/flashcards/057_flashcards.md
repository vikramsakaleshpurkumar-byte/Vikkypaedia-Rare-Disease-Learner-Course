### Card 14 (Molecular Etiology)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> Pathogenic variants in **`NLRP12`** are associated with which clinical phenotype and molecular mechanism in **NLRP12-Associated Autoinflammatory Disease/Guadeloupe Variant Periodic Fever Syndrome (FCAS2)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Recurrent fevers associated with NLRP12 variants involving twins from Guadeloupe who experienced symptoms such as arthralgia, periodic fever, and cold-induced urticaria and was named the Guadeloupe Variant Periodic Fever Syndrome, also known as FCAS2 (Familial Cold Autoinflammatory Syndrome 2).

</details>

### Card 15 (Molecular Etiology)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> Pathogenic variants in **`NLRC4`** are associated with which clinical phenotype and molecular mechanism in **Autoinflammation With Infantile Enterocolitis (AIFEC)/NLRC4-AID**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Heterozygous gain-of-function variants in NLRC4 (NODlike family caspase activation and recruitment domaincontaining protein-4) result in a syndrome of HLH/MAS, severe intestinal inflammation, skin rash, and fever.

</details>

### Card 16 (Molecular Etiology)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> Pathogenic variants in **`MVK`** are associated with which clinical phenotype and molecular mechanism in **Mevalonate Kinase Deficiency/ Hyperimmunoglobulinemia D and Periodic Fever Syndrome**?</summary>

**Answer:**
The enzyme mevalonate kinase (MVK) is widely expressed throughout the body and has a crucial role in a key step of the biosynthetic pathway for both cholesterol and nonsterol isoprenes.

</details>

### Card 26 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the clinical evaluation of **Autoinflammation With Infantile Enterocolitis (AIFEC)/NLRC4-AID**, how is **A severe phenotype (NLRC4-AID phenotype 1)** defined and characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
A severe phenotype (NLRC4-AID phenotype 1) with neonatal onset and a high mortality rate (40%), presenting with high fevers, macrophage activation syndrome (MAS), severe enterocolitis, and a nonspecific skin eruption.

</details>

### Card 27 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)

**Answer:**
A mild phenotype (NLRC4-AID phenotype 2) with onset in childhood, a cutaneous tropism with urticaria and erythematous nodules, arthromyalgia, and conjunctivitis.

</details>
