### Card 22 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the clinical evaluation of **Febrile Seizures (FS)**, how is **complex FS** defined and characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
complex FS is a focal seizure, a generalized seizure lasting longer than 15 minutes, the recurrence of seizures more than once in 24 hours, or a seizure associated with postictal neurologic abnormalities, most frequently a postictal (Todd) paralysis, occurring in the context of a fever.

</details>

### Card 23 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the clinical evaluation of **Febrile Seizures (FS)**, how is **prolonged febrile seizure** defined and characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
prolonged febrile seizure or febrile status epilepticus (FSE) is defined as a seizure occurring in the context of a fever and lasting longer than 30 minutes.

</details>
