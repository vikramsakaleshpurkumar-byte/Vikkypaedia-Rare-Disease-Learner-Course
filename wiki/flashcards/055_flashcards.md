### Card 22 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the clinical evaluation of **Genetic Etiologies of Papillomaviruses (HPV) Infections in Humans**, how is **Epidermodysplasia verruciformis (EV)** defined and characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Epidermodysplasia verruciformis (EV) is a rare genodermatosis presenting with persistent, disseminated, flat warts and pityriasis versicolor-like skin lesions induced by human β-papillomaviruses (β-HPVs).

### Card 23 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the clinical evaluation of **Genetic Etiologies of Papillomaviruses (HPV) Infections in Humans**, how is **Juvenile recurrent respiratory papillomatosis** defined and characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Juvenile recurrent respiratory papillomatosis , driven by some α-HPVs, was shown to be due to biallelic gainof-function (GOF) in NLRP1, suggesting an important role of cell intrinsic immunity against HPVs other than β-HPVs (α, γ, μ and ν).

</details>
