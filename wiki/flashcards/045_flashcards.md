### Card 17 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the clinical evaluation of **Metabolic Crisis With Inborn Errors of Metabolism**, how is **metabolic crisis** defined and characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
metabolic crisis is the result of insufficiently functioning, or completely nonfunctioning, enzymes that lead to a deficiency of metabolites necessary for cellular function and to an accumulation of toxic metabolites that further perturb metabolism.

</details>

### Card 26 (Visual & Imaging Spotter)
![](../../figures/fig_p1020_07.jpg)

<details><summary><b>Q:</b> Identify the diagnostic sign, dysmorphic phenotype, neuroimaging pattern, or molecular pathway illustrated below (Fig. 45.2):</summary>

**Answer:**
Branched chain amino acid metabolism. Some reactions are not shown. Odd-chain fatty acids (i.e., propionate), threonine, and methionine also are catabolized to propionyl-CoA via alternate pathways. Branched chain amino transferase (blue box) has not been officially attributed to disease. Enzymes in red boxes are related to disease. Branched chain alpha-ketoacid dehydrogenase is a shared enzyme that metabolizes the three essential branched chain amino acids, leucine, isoleucine, and valine. Leucine is further metabolized via a pathway involving the enzyme responsible for IVA, isovaleryl-CoA dehydrogenase. Isoleucine and valine have a shared catabolic pathway involving the enzymes related to PA, propionyl-CoA carboxylase and MMA, methylmalonyl-CoA epimerase and mutase.

</details>
