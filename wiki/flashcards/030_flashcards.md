### Card 21 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the clinical evaluation of **Vascular and Lymphatic Malformations**, how is **Vascular tumors** defined and characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Vascular tumors , most commonly infantile hemangiomas, are lesions that have proliferative growth of endothelial cells and pericytes, and are divided into benign, locally aggressive, or borderline, and malignant.

</details>

### Card 22 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)

**Answer:**
Vascular malformations are localized defects of vascular morphogenesis during embryogenesis, composed of dysplastic vascular channels.
