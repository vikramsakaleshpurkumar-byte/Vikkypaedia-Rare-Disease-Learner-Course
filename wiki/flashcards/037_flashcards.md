### Card 17 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the clinical evaluation of **Monogenic Etiologies of Hypertension YOSUKE MIYASHITA, JOSEPH T. FLYNN**, how is **Primary hypertension** defined and characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Primary hypertension does not have an identifiable etiology, and although genetic factors may play a role, it is largely attributable to body habitus, family history, and lifestyle factors.

</details>

### Card 19 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the clinical evaluation of **Gordon Syndrome**, how is **familial hyperkalemia and hypertension syndrome** defined and characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
familial hyperkalemia and hypertension syndrome , is a form of low-renin, low-aldosterone monogenic hypertension that presents with hyperkalemia , in contrast to the low or normal levels of serum potassium seen in patients with most of other forms of monogenic hypertension.

</details>

### Card 20 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the clinical evaluation of **Syndrome of Apparent Mineralocorticoid Excess**, how is **Type 1 AME** defined and characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Type 1 AME is more severe due to complete absence of 11βHSD2 activity and typically presents early in life with hypertension, low plasma renin and aldosterone levels, hypokalemia, metabolic alkalosis, and an elevated urinary cortisol:cortisone ratio.

</details>

### Card 21 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the clinical evaluation of **Syndrome of Apparent Mineralocorticoid Excess**, how is **Type 2 AME** defined and characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Type 2 AME is milder due to varying degrees of residual 11βHSD2 enzyme activity and presents later in life with lower-grade hypertension than in type 1 AME; electrolyte abnormalities are rare or absent.

</details>

familial hyperaldosteronism type 1 , aldosterone synthase hyperactivity, and glucocorticoid suppressible hyperaldosteronism.

</details>
