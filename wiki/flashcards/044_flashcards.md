<details><summary><b>Q:</b> Identify the diagnostic sign, dysmorphic phenotype, neuroimaging pattern, or molecular pathway illustrated below (Fig. 44.1):</summary>

**Answer:**
Simplified schematic illustration of chemical neurotransmission. Neurotransmitters (NT) are synthesized (1) in the presynaptic neuron, transported into the synaptic vesicle (2), and stored in the synaptic vesicle (3) until a depolarization signal triggers release of the NT (4). Once enough NT accumulates in the synaptic cleft, postsynaptic receptors (R) are activated (5), triggering a number of postsynaptic neuron modifications (6) (depolarization, long term synaptic plasticity, intracellular signal transduction). NT reuptake (7) occurs through membrane bound NT transporters into the presynaptic neuron, where it will be either degraded/ catabolized (8) or re-utilized by storage in the synaptic vesicle (9).

</details>
