### Card 16 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the clinical evaluation of **Dysplasias vs. Dysostoses**, how is **Dysplasias** defined and characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Dysplasias are disorders of skeletal growth; they tend to affect all skeletal elements, and are progressive, because they continue to evolve throughout childhood and adolescence.

</details>

### Card 17 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the clinical evaluation of **Dysplasias vs. Dysostoses**, how is **collagen type 1** defined and characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
collagen type 1 (the main collagen of bone ) and collagen type 2 (the main collagen of cartilage ).

</details>

### Card 18 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the clinical evaluation of **Therapeutic Approaches**, how is **Kniest dysplasia** defined and characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Kniest dysplasia is essentially a severe SEDC variant with hyperplastic (but unossified) epiphyses; it is often associated with type II collagen exon-skipping variants or small deletions.

</details>

### Card 19 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the clinical evaluation of **Therapeutic Approaches**, how is **Stickler syndrome** defined and characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
Stickler syndrome that combines hearing loss, myopia of variable degree, cleft palate or uvula, and normal or even tall stature.

</details>

### Card 21 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the clinical evaluation of **Lysosomal Storage Disorders and the Dysostosis Multiplex Phenotype (See Chapter 46)**, how is **mucopolysaccharidoses** defined and characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
mucopolysaccharidoses are systemic metabolic disorders; in their full-blown forms, they affect connective tissues (skin, joints, eyes, heart valves, and more), the skeleton (with dysostosis multiplex), the airways, the inner ear, the liver and spleen, and the brain with intellectual disability.

</details>

### Card 22 (Clinical & Diagnostic Concept)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> In the clinical evaluation of **Lysosomal Storage Disorders and the Dysostosis Multiplex Phenotype (See Chapter 46)**, how is **mucolipidosis 2** defined and characterized?</summary>

**Answer:**
mucolipidosis 2 and mucolipidosis 3 ; they are allelic conditions related to GNPTAB and GNPTG , necessary for the targeting of lysosomal enzymes to the lysosome.

</details>
