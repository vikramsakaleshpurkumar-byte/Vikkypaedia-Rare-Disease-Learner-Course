### Card 9 (Molecular Etiology)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> Pathogenic variants in **`SPTA1`** are associated with which clinical phenotype and molecular mechanism in **Hereditary Spherocytosis**?</summary>

**Answer:**
The most severe form of HS is a compound heterozygous condition wherein a pathogenic SPTA1 variant coexists in trans with the null polymorphic α-LEPRA (Low Expression, Prague) allele, which allows for residual expression of small amounts of normal α-spectrin sufficient to avoid the lethality associated with complete absence of α-spectrin.

</details>
