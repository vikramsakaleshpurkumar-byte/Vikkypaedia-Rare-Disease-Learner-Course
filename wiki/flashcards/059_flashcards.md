### Card 23 (Visual & Imaging Spotter)
![](../../figures/fig_p1322_01.jpg)

<details><summary><b>Q:</b> Identify the diagnostic sign, dysmorphic phenotype, neuroimaging pattern, or molecular pathway illustrated below (Fig. 59.1):</summary>

**Answer:**
The effect of known IPEX and IPEX-like associated gene defects on Treg (regulatory T cells) generation and function. Both AIRE and FOXP3 play important roles in the generation of Treg cells in the thymus. The expansion, maintenance, and activation of Treg cells in the periphery is known to be highly dependent on interleukin-2 (IL-2), which is required to maintain long-term expression of FOXP3. Variants in the IL-2 receptor chains ( IL2RA / IL2RB ) or in STAT5B affect this aspect of the Treg function. Treg cells mediate immune suppression via a variety of mechanisms including downregulation of antigen presenting cell co-stimulatory function by CTLA-4 and through secretion of immunoregulatory cytokines like interleukin-10 (IL-10) and transforming growth factor-β (TGF-β). Variants in genes that affect CTLA-4 expression (CTLA4, LRBA, DEF6) or expression/responsiveness to IL-10 (IL10, IL10RA, IL10RB) affect these functions of Tregs. Lastly it is not yet known whether gain-of-function variants in STAT1 or STAT3 ( STAT1-GOF or STAT3-GOF ) affect the responsiveness of effector cells to Treg suppression or directly affect Treg function.

</details>
