### Card 7 (Syndrome & OMIM Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the genetic etiology, inheritance pattern, and hallmark clinical features of **X-linked lymphoproliferative disorder type 1 (XLP1)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Causative Gene(s):** `SH2D1A, SH2, XLP1` · **Inheritance:** X-Linked

**Clinical & Molecular Profile:** Gene or Locus (Protein): SH2D1A(SH2 Domain Containing 1A) — HLH-Related Dysfunction: Defective killing of EBV-infected B cells — Unique Clinical Features: Severe HLH; Hypogammaglobulinemia, and lymphoma

</details>

### Card 8 (Syndrome & OMIM Profile)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> What are the genetic etiology, inheritance pattern, and hallmark clinical features of **X-linked lymphoproliferative disorder type 2 (XLP2)**?</summary>

**Answer:**
**Causative Gene(s):** `XIAP, XLP2` · **Inheritance:** X-Linked

**Clinical & Molecular Profile:** Gene or Locus (Protein): XIAP(X-linked inhibitor of apoptosis protein) — HLH-Related Dysfunction: Increased inhibition of apoptosis in EBV-infected cells (gain of function) — Unique Clinical Features: Mild, recurrent HLH (may be triggered by non-EBV viruses)

</details>

### Card 11 (Molecular Etiology)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> Pathogenic variants in **`BRAF`** are associated with which clinical phenotype and molecular mechanism in **Langerhans Cell Histiocytosis**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Mitogen-activated protein kinase (MAPK) pathway gene alterations are present in more than 85% of cases of LCH (most commonly BRAF -V600E pathogenic variants), and activation of this pathway appears to be a primary inducer of lymphoproliferation, suggestive of a neoplastic process.

</details>

### Card 16 (Molecular Etiology)
