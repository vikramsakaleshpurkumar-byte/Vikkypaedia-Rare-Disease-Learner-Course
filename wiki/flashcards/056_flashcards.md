### Card 12 (Molecular Etiology)
<details><summary><b>Q:</b> Pathogenic variants in **`STAT3`** are associated with which clinical phenotype and molecular mechanism in **Syndrome**?</summary>

**Answer:**
Most cases of ADHIES are due to dominant negative pathogenic variants in the signal transducer and activator of transcription 3 (STAT3) gene, leading to loss of function and failure of Th17 cell differentiation and reduced IL-17 and IL-22 production.

</details>

### Card 16 (Molecular Etiology)

### Card 19 (Precision Therapy & Management)

**Answer:**
**Therapy / Intervention:** Targeted / Disease-Modifying Therapy

**Clinical Context:** The treatment of CAPS is focused on reducing IL-1β activity using medications like anakinra (IL-1 receptor antagonist), rilonacept (IL-1 trap), and canakinumab (IL-1β monoclonal antibody).
